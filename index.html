One-time payment. No subscription and no auto-renewal. The playbook is 12 months of access — it stops at expiry with no charge; the system is yours to keep once you create a copy.

How soon can I start after paying?

Usually within minutes — the access email goes to the address you entered at checkout. If nothing arrives in 15 minutes, check spam first, then email support@knowpay.app.

Is it suitable for complete beginners?

Yes. The playbook starts from Positioning and assumes no platform jargon; the system needs a Feishu/Lark account — filling it in is easier than learning it.
