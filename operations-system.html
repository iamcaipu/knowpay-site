What this is

An operations system that runs on Feishu/Lark Base. After purchase you receive a template link; create a copy and it's yours, forever.

It includes four business modules, two reports, a user guide, and demo data — copy it over and you see the complete working picture, then replace it with your own numbers.

The key design tradeoff: we deliberately didn't build the full chain

A full chain sounds complete; the price is manual entry at every step. The more fields, the fewer people filling them — the tables end up empty, and an empty system is no system. So we kept only core actions and core data, organized around money and assets: how money goes out (purchasing, first-mile freight, stocking costs, operating costs), how it comes back (revenue), what it's sitting on (inventory assets), and how it all nets out (two reports).

A system you'll actually fill in, read, and stick with — that's the only kind that counts as useful.

Why it's designed this way

Before this version, we tried the "manage everything" build: orders, logistics, after-sales, creators, ads — all of it. The result: too many fields, too much entry, operators quit after two weeks, and the data became decoration.

This version answers four questions only:

  1. What did this batch of goods cost in total?
  2. How much money is sitting in the warehouse right now?
  3. This month — how much revenue, how much cost?
  4. What did we actually make?

Answer those four clearly and the business won't run away from you. Everything else lives in the platform backend already — no need to copy it over.

FAQ

Do I need to know formulas or Base?

No coding. Day to day it's filling in and reading; the guide explains every field and how to fill it.

Where is my data stored?

In your own Feishu/Lark account. Once you create a copy, the data belongs to your workspace — we can't see it or get it.

Can it manage order details?

By design, no. Orders live in the TikTok Shop backend; this system records monthly revenue totals and doesn't duplicate orders.

Can my team use it too?

Yes. The copy sits in your account — invite teammates under Feishu/Lark's own permission rules.

If you upgrade the template, do I get the new version?

Not automatically. Copy and original are independent systems; upgrades are sold as new products.

Does it work on both Feishu and Lark?

Yes. It's built on Base, which both Feishu and Lark support. If your account tier differs in features, the platform's actual offering applies.

Why no refunds?
