2. What we collect

2.1 Information you provide

  • Order information: name and email address, used to deliver products and send order-related notices.
  • Payment information: transaction amount, order number and payment status. We do not store full card numbers; card data is handled by the payment processor (see Section 5).
  • Communications: the content of emails you send for support, after-sales or refunds.

2.2 Information collected automatically

  • Device and network: IP address, browser type, operating system and language.
  • Access records: page visits and basic traffic statistics.
  • Log data: request timestamps and error logs, used to keep the site running properly.

We do not collect location data or biometric data, we do not offer third-party social login, and we do not operate user accounts.

5. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell your personal information, including as "sale" is defined under laws such as the CCPA. We share only in these cases:

  • Business transfers: in a merger or acquisition, with prior notice and continuity of protection obligations.
  • With your consent: for other purposes, with your explicit consent.

6. Data security

  • Encryption in transit: HTTPS / TLS site-wide.
  • Access control: order data is accessible only to the operator.
  • No card storage: card data never enters our servers.
  • Data minimization: we collect only what is necessary to deliver the product.

If a security incident affects your rights, we will notify you and the relevant regulators within 72 hours of becoming aware, as required by law.
