KnowPay: cross-border knowledge, delivered

Knowledge you can count on.

KnowPay focuses on one vertical: the cross-border e-commerce track. We deliver what actually matters in it — the roads we've walked, written as a playbook you can follow, and the money, the stock and the numbers gathered into a system you can keep filling in.

We're not a general knowledge platform. Everything here comes from hands-on operations in this trade: first get the work right, then deliver the method. Knowledge is worth paying for — but only if it's actually usable. So we don't run a course vending machine, we don't sell "guaranteed sales" fantasies, and we don't repackage public material and resell it to you.

What we do

  • TikTok Shop Playbook (2026 Edition): nine stages from Positioning to Review, organized in the order you actually operate, with acceptance standards for every step. Read online, 12 months of access.
  • Feishu/Lark-Based Operations System: purchasing, first-mile freight, stocking costs, operating costs, revenue, inventory assets and reports — copy and use, yours forever.

What we don't do

  • No agency or done-for-you store services
  • No promises of sales, income or rankings
  • No tickets, gift cards, accounts or similar categories
  • No fake reviews or fabricated data

Where the content comes from

We've run cross-border stores continuously since 2023. The playbook and the system are built from our own operating records, reviews and mistakes. Every method in them was validated on our own stores; anything we haven't validated or can't explain how to execute, we leave out.

Anything at all — purchasing, refunds, content questions, invoices — email support@knowpay.app. We reply within 2 business days.
